Packets the field node sends to the cloud over LoRa / GSM (simulated).

Sensor data is simulated. Every decision comes from the Kisan Mitra API, so real ESP32 readings can replace the simulator later. How it works: water use = reference evapotranspiration (Hargreaves) × crop coefficient (FAO-56) + field losses; irrigation is advised when the forecast drops below the crop's refill point within 24 h and no useful rain (8 mm) is due. Fertiliser doses come from the gap to the crop-stage target band, capped per application, and are held back when 15 mm of rain is due within 48 h. Crop parameters are illustrative and Telugu text awaits native-speaker review. Impact: 0.2 kWh per 1,000 L pumped (≈30 m lift), 0.71 kg CO₂ per kWh, 4.2 kg CO₂e per kg urea.
